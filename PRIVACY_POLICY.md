## 2. Personal Data We Collect & How We Use It

### A. Appointment Alert Subscriptions (Email & Telegram)
When you sign up to receive free notification alerts for new Bürgeramt registration slots:
- We collect your **Email Address** and/or your **Telegram @username**.
- **Purpose:** To send you automated instant notifications when new appointment slots become available.
- **Legal Basis:** Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (Consent). You can unsubscribe at any time.

### B. Payment Processing (Stripe Integration)
If you purchase premium services or checkouts:
- Credit card details, billing addresses, and payment tokens are processed directly by our third-party payment provider, **Stripe**.
- We do not store or see your full credit card information on our servers.
- **Legal Basis:** Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (Performance of a contract).

### C. Anmeldung Registration Form PDF Generator
Our portal allows you to pre-fill the official German registration form (`Anmeldeformular`) and download it as a filled PDF:
- The data entered (Name, Date of Birth, Passport details, Previous address, Landlord details, etc.) is processed in server memory to generate the PDF download.
- We **do not store** your filled registration form details in a permanent database. The data is wiped as soon as the PDF stream is successfully delivered to your browser.
- **Legal Basis:** Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (Contractual service requested by the user).

## 4. Your Rights under GDPR
Under the GDPR, you have the following rights:
- **Right to Access (Art. 15):** Request a copy of your stored data.
- **Right to Rectification (Art. 16):** Correct inaccurate data.
- **Right to Erasure (Art. 17):** Request complete deletion of your data (e.g. email alerts list).
- **Right to Withdraw Consent (Art. 7(3)):** Unsubscribe from notifications at any time.

To exercise these rights, please email: henrysaahgomez@outlook.es.
